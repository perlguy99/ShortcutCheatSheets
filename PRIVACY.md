# Privacy Policy — Shortcut Cheat Sheets

**Last updated: October 2026**

Shortcut Cheat Sheets does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your device

Your shortcut collections, categories, favorites, hidden items, and app settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. Nothing here is ever sent anywhere — not to us, not to any third party.

## What the app does send a network request for

To let you browse and import shortcut collections for other apps, the app fetches public JSON files from a GitHub-hosted library (`raw.githubusercontent.com/perlguy99/ShortcutCheatSheets`). These requests are unauthenticated and carry no personal data, account identifiers, or device identifiers — they're the same as visiting any public web page.

## The Mac exporter tool

The companion Mac app (used to generate a shortcut collection from an app on your own Mac) reads menu bar data from the app you point it at, using macOS's Accessibility API, which requires your explicit permission. It writes a JSON file to your own Mac — it does not transmit anything over the network, to us or anyone else.

## Printing

When you generate a PDF cheat sheet, it's created and saved entirely on your device. We have no way to see what you print.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
